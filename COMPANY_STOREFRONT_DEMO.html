🔗 Navigation Structure

  • Main Marketplace: http://localhost:3000/ - Shows products from ALL companies
  • Company Directory: http://localhost:3000/companies - Lists all company stores
  • Individual Stores:
    • http://localhost:3000/company/1 - Vintage Treasures only
    • http://localhost:3000/company/2 - Eco Fashion Hub only
    • http://localhost:3000/company/3 - Retro Style Co only

✅ How It Works

  1. Company Registration: Companies sign up via admin panel and get immediate access
  2. Product Management: Each company adds products through their admin dashboard
  3. Data Isolation: Products are linked to company_id in database
  4. Customer Experience:
    • Visit /companies to see all stores
    • Click on a company to visit their dedicated storefront
    • Each storefront shows only that company's products
    • Same shopping cart/wishlist functionality per store
