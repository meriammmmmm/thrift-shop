HOW TO GET YOUR SHOP BACK ON A FREE DATABASE
=============================================

You have everything you need in this folder. Follow these steps slowly,
one at a time. You do NOT have to pay.

WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
---------------------
- products_rows.csv ........ the original full backup (with images inside it)
- products_clean.csv ....... light version, opens in Excel, images = filenames
- product_images/ .......... all 148 product photos as real .jpg files
- 01_create_products_table.sql .. the file that builds your table
- HOW_TO_RESTORE.txt ....... this file


STEP 1 — MAKE A NEW FREE PROJECT
--------------------------------
1. Go to supabase.com and log in.
2. Click "New project".
3. Pick your free organization, give it a name (e.g. mery-rose-clothing-2),
   choose a password, click Create. Wait ~2 minutes for it to be ready.

   ** IMPORTANT: this new project is FREE, same as before. **


STEP 2 — BUILD THE TABLE
------------------------
1. In the new project, click "SQL Editor" on the left.
2. Open the file 01_create_products_table.sql, copy ALL of it.
3. Paste it into the SQL Editor and click RUN.
   You now have an empty "products" table.


STEP 3 — PUT THE PRODUCTS IN
----------------------------
1. Click "Table Editor" on the left, open the "products" table.
2. Click "Insert" -> "Import data from CSV".
3. Choose products_clean.csv from this folder. Import it.
   Your 78 products are now in the new database.


STEP 4 — PUT THE PHOTOS IN  (this is the part that keeps it free)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Click "Storage" on the left -> "New bucket".
2. Name it exactly:  product-images   and make it Public. Create.
3. Open the bucket, click Upload, and upload everything inside the
   product_images folder.
4. Your photos now live as files (cheap) instead of inside the database.

   The "images" column already lists the matching filenames for each
   product, so your app can find them.


WHY THE OLD ONE BROKE
---------------------
The old database stored each photo as a giant block of text inside the
database itself. That filled up the free limit fast. Storing photos as
files in Storage (Step 4) keeps the database tiny, so the free plan is
plenty.

If you get stuck on any step, come back to the chat and tell me which
step number you're on.
